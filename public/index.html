Score weights

Weights are normalized to sum to 100%. At least one must be positive.

How the score works

Each component is scaled to 0–100 across the current twelve sites. More population and spending score higher. Fewer rivals and lower first-year site costs score higher.

First-year site cost
Investment + 12 × monthly rent.

Equal inputs receive a neutral score of 50. Scores are relative to this candidate set.

The radius includes whole zones whose centroids fall inside a straight-line circle. Roads do not influence this version.
